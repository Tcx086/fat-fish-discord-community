# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Critique ideas and code without
personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment,
impersonation and disclosure of another person's private information are not
acceptable. Do not share recordings, personal conversations or voice likenesses
without the necessary permission. Respect a participant's refusal or withdrawal.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and project-operated
community spaces. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, restrict
participation or ban repeated or serious misconduct, proportionate to the impact.
Reports should be handled discreetly; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when
safety or other obligations require disclosure. A maintainer involved in a report
should recuse themselves where another responsible maintainer is available.

The maintainer must name a private conduct-reporting contact before public launch.
No unverified address or response-time promise is supplied here. Do not post a
sensitive report publicly while that contact is pending. Participants may ask a
non-involved maintainer to reconsider a moderation decision.

This is a project-specific policy, not a claim of adoption of an external template.
